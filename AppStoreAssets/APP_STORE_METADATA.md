## Description

Recast is a private, multi-format conversion workspace for quick everyday jobs and demanding professional workflows. Drop or choose files, select an output format, and let Recast determine a supported conversion route using Apple’s native frameworks.

CONVERT MORE TYPES OF WORK
• Images: prepare photos and graphics for sharing, publishing, archiving, and the web
• Documents and PDFs: create portable outputs for clients, classmates, teams, and records
• Audio and video: produce compatible media outputs with clear progress and quality controls
• Structured data: move between practical text and data formats such as CSV and JSON
• Mixed queues: keep different file jobs together in one understandable workspace

SMART CONVERSION ROUTING
Some formats do not have a direct path. Recast’s capability graph can identify a supported multi-step route and coordinate the intermediate work for you. You see the chosen source and destination while Recast manages the details.

BUILT FOR EVERYDAY USERS
Convert iPhone images before sharing, turn notes into portable documents, prepare media for a presentation, or standardize downloaded files without uploading them to an unknown website.

BUILT FOR PROFESSIONALS
Queue batches, process folders, tune image and video quality, resize output, preserve metadata when supported, apply predictable naming, and review individual job status. Recast is useful for designers, developers, researchers, operations teams, content producers, and anyone who repeatedly prepares files for other systems.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Compatible App Store conversions run locally on your device. Recast does not upload your files, collect their contents, or require an account. Your source material stays under your control.

FREE TO USE
Recast is completely free to download and use. Every compatible converter and feature is unlocked from first launch, with no account, no sign-in, and no In-App Purchases.

Recast 1.1 adds a clear free plan, optional monthly and annual Premium subscriptions, one-week trial eligibility, purchase restoration, subscription management, verified StoreKit entitlements, and thoughtfully timed Premium and review prompts.
