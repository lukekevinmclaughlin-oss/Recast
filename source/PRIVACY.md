# Recast Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 12, 2026

Recast processes files locally on your device. The developer does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data, file contents, filenames, conversion history, analytics, advertising identifiers, or diagnostics.

Files are accessed only when you select or drop them. Recast uses Apple system frameworks to perform App Store conversions on-device and writes results to locations you choose. Subscription purchases are processed by Apple; the developer does not receive your payment-card details.

Recast does not use tracking technologies or third-party advertising SDKs. You can remove local preferences and conversion history by deleting the app. Questions may be submitted through the repository’s Support page.
